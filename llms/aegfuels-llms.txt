# AEG Fuels (Associated Energy Group, LLC)

> Global aviation fuel supply, logistics and trip-support company, Miami, Florida, founded
> 1988. Arranges jet fuel uplifts at 3,000+ locations in 197 countries for commercial air
> carriers, business and general aviation, FBOs and government aircraft, plus flight
> support, fuel management, tax recovery/VAT exemption, a Sustainable Aviation Fuel
> program, marine fuel, land fuel, the Carnet fuel card, AEG Rewards, and the AEG Connect
> FBO network.

IMPORTANT: AEG Fuels publishes NO public API, developer portal, SDK, webhook catalog or
machine-readable contract. Verified 2026-09-09 by probing every host the record knows
(aegfuels.com, www.aegfuels.com, app.aegfuels.com, portal.aegrewards.com,
paygateway.aegfuels.com) for /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json,
/swagger/v1/swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc, /graphql, /llms.txt,
/apis.json, /apis.yml and the full named /.well-known/ path list. Every path returned 404,
500 or a connection failure. There is no GitHub organization and no package on npm or
PyPI. Customer transactions happen inside the authenticated MILO client portal and the
mobile apps, not over a published interface. Do not attempt to construct or call an AEG
Fuels API endpoint; there is none to call.
